Soil To Soul

Why We Founded Mitti Se

In our grandparents' generation, heart ailments, diabetes, and autoimmune issues were rare. Food wasn't just calories—it was medicine (Aahar hi Aushadhi hai). But over the last 40 years, ultra-processed industrial food, chemically deodorized oils, and mass-market flours stripped away our health.

Mitti Se was born with a single mission: to reconnect urban families directly with the sacred soil of India. We partner directly with regenerative farmer collectives across Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Kerala, paying them fair guaranteed premiums while delivering 100% pure, unadulterated nourishment to your dining table.
